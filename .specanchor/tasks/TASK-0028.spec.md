# Task: A style catalogue the client can look at

## 1. Problem / requested change

Owner request: when the client names a style, show three examples of it and let them pick one;
the pick then feeds the final design alongside their own uploads and anything the scout found.
Implements ADR-0012. Second of three tasks, after TASK-0027 widened the vocabulary.

## 2. Current behavior

- A style is settled from a word. `tribal` covers at least three families that look nothing
  alike, and the client is asked to imagine which one they mean. Evidence: OBSERVED.
- `STYLE_PHRASES` lives in `flash/prompt.py`, reachable only from Python. Evidence: OBSERVED.
- No catalogue, no assets, and `apps/web/public/` does not exist. Evidence: OBSERVED.

## 3. Desired behavior

Sixteen styles, three described and illustrated variants each. The client picks by looking. The
pick is recorded as a reference with honest provenance.

## 4. Scope

`contracts/reference/style-library.json` and its export, `services/worker/generation/style_library.py`,
`services/worker/app/build_style_library.py`, `services/worker/flash/prompt.py`,
`packages/consultation/src/agents/style-library.ts` and its tests and exports,
`apps/web/src/components/StylePicker.tsx`, `apps/web/src/app/page.tsx`,
`apps/web/src/app/api/consultation/route.ts`, `globals.css`, and the 48 generated assets.

## 5. Out of scope

The master-prompt review gate: the third task. No change to how a design is generated from a
brief, or to the mockup.

## 6. Affected anchors

ADR-0012 (implements), ADR-0009 (FLUX.2 renders skin, which is what a catalogue wants),
ADR-0004 (one source of truth, applied to the style phrases), ARCH-INV-001, PROD-INV-004,
WEB-INV-001, CONSULT-INV-003.

## 7. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — Every style in the vocabulary has three described variants in shared contract data.
- **REQ-002** — The style phrase is held once and read by `flash/prompt.py`.
- **REQ-003** — A generator produces one catalogue image per variant, downscaled and committed.
  It lives in `generation`, takes an injected provider, collects failures, and is resumable.
- **REQ-004** — The consultation turns a style into offers and an offer into a `ReferenceImage`
  marked `style_library`.
- **REQ-005** — A pick is resolved server-side from an identifier; anything unresolvable is
  refused.
- **REQ-006** — The interface shows the three variants and says they are illustrative renders,
  not photographs of real work.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** (REQ-001) — Every vocabulary style yields exactly three offers with distinct ids; an
  unknown style yields none.
- **AC-002** (REQ-002) — The existing vocabulary-coverage test passes with the phrases sourced
  from the catalogue.
- **AC-003** (REQ-003) — 48 images exist, one per variant, each under 150 KiB; the architecture
  tests still pass, proving no cycle and no egress outside the permitted modules.
- **AC-004** (REQ-004) — A pick is `style_library`, never `user_supplied`, and carries the
  catalogue path and a licence describing it as a studio render.
- **AC-005** (REQ-005) — A fabricated identifier is refused.
- **AC-006** (REQ-006) — The picker renders the three options with the disclaimer; the client
  bundle builds without pulling server-only modules.

## 9. Design approach

See ADR-0012. Two constraints shaped the structure more than anything else: outbound calls are
confined to `generation`, and `generation` may not import `app`. That fixes the generator in
`generation` with an injected provider and the runner in `app`.

## 10. API / data / UI impact

One new BFF action, `style_variant`, taking an identifier only. One new canonical data file. 48
static assets, 3.7 MB. `ReferenceImage.verification` gains `style_library`.

## 11. Migration and backward compatibility

Additive. A session with no pick behaves exactly as before.

## 12. Test plan

Offer and provenance logic is unit-tested. The images themselves are reviewed by eye, because no
test can say whether a render represents a style; a sample was inspected rather than all 48.

## 13. Risks and rollback

The catalogue is renders presented to someone making a permanent decision. The mitigation is the
disclaimer, which is weaker than it sounds. Rollback is removing the picker; the assets are inert.

## 15. Decision log

- **DEC-001** — The generator could not live in `scripts/`. ARCH-INV-001 confines outbound model
  calls to `generation` and `safety`, and a source-scanning test enforces it. The obvious home was
  the forbidden one.
- **DEC-002** — The provider is injected. Building it inside `generation` would have meant
  importing `app`, closing a cycle the architecture test rejects.
- **DEC-003** — The style phrase moved out of `flash/prompt.py` into shared data. Writing separate
  catalogue prompts would have let the picture a client chose drift from the tattoo they are sold.
- **DEC-004** — Importing the consultation index into the client bundle broke the build:
  `node:crypto` reached webpack through the orchestrator. Exposed on a narrow `./style-library`
  subpath, mirroring the existing `./preferences`.
- **DEC-005** — Two variant descriptions were refused repeatedly by the provider's content filter:
  *"children's-book line"* next to "bare adult skin", and *"anchor, swallow and rose"*. Both
  styles are entirely benign and the wording was ambiguous out of context, so both were reworded
  and passed immediately. Reworded, not circumvented; had they failed again they would have
  shipped without an image.
