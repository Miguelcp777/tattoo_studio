# Task: Consent at sign-in, and pop-ups instead of checkboxes

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner: the steps are confusing (body type, the age and permission checkboxes); permissions
should be granted on entering with general terms that protect the studio; unsaved preferences
should be a «¿Quiere guardar preferencias?» pop-up; "todo se haga por pop up de aceptación". Choices:
a draft of the legal texts by the developer, the body asked in a pop-up when generating, a save
pop-up, and one final pop-up. Company: Aurevanta Labs, info@aurevanta.es. Evidence: INTENT.

## 2. Current behavior

Generation required age, consent and references-reviewed checkboxes, an accepted brief («Aceptar y
continuar») and saved preferences; uploads and the camera try-on had their own checkboxes. Nothing
recorded consent (SAFETY-INV-003). Evidence: OBSERVED.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — Signing in requires accepting the current terms and image consent, shown as two
  sections in one dialog with one button; refused without them before Supabase is called.
- **REQ-002** — The acceptance is bound to the account and the text versions, cleared on sign-out,
  and recorded in the sign-in event.
- **REQ-003** — Uploads, generation and kept camera photos require it (428); an open session without
  it is asked in the same dialog.
- **REQ-004** — No consent or acceptance checkbox remains; «Generar» asks, as pop-ups and only when
  they apply, to save unsaved changes, for the body, and to confirm the summary.
- **REQ-005** — The full terms and privacy policy are readable before signing in.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — `auth/route.test.ts`: no or old acceptance → 400, Supabase never called; accepted →
  `inkcraft_ok` set, httpOnly; sign-out clears it; `GET` reports `consented`.
- **AC-002** — `telemetry.test.ts`: the sign-in event carries `detail: {terms, images}`.
- **AC-003** — `consent/route.test.ts`: without consent, upload and generate answer 428 without
  reaching the worker; `/api/consent` accepts only current versions and only for a signed-in
  account; one account's cookie does not serve another. `captures/route.test.ts`: 428 likewise.
- **AC-004** — `generation-flow.test.ts`: save, then body, then always confirm; the body does not
  block the button. `next-step`/`steps` tests: no consent blockers, no permissions step.
- **AC-005** — `middleware.test.ts`: `/condiciones` and `/privacidad` are public.
- **AC-006** — Live: an old session sees the dialog and, accepted, the studio has no checkbox; a
  sign-in shows «Antes de entrar» before any session exists and «Acepto y entro» lands in the
  studio, consented; «Generar» with an unsaved change asks to save, then for the body, then shows
  the summary with the saved values.
