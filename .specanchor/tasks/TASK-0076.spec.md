# Task: Progress shows only what the server reports (audit UX-03)

## 1. Problem / requested change

Audit finding UX-03: the progress lines and bar of TASK-0069 advanced on a clock and could announce
«retoques» while the worker was elsewhere. The audit asked for the real step from the worker,
until then an indeterminate indicator and the time, the place in the queue, and the option to come
back later. Evidence: OBSERVED in `lib/ink-progress.ts` (by the auditor and here).

## 2. Current behavior

`inkStage(task, elapsedMs)` chose a line by time and drew an asymptotic bar; the worker reported
only `queued` or `running`.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — Contract: `studio-status` gains optional `stage` (`references`, `skin`, `drawing`,
  `stencil`, `placing`, `finishing`) and `queuePosition` (integer ≥ 1).
- **REQ-002** — Worker: the queue records the step reported by the job running in its thread
  (`JobQueue.stage`), from the reference analysis, each generation-graph node (`on_node`) and a
  re-placement; `get` adds it to a running job and adds the place in the queue to a queued one.
- **REQ-003** — Web: a running design shows «paso N de 6» and that step's line with a bar at that
  step; without a step, «Empezando…» and an indeterminate bar; a queued one says how many are ahead;
  every other wait shows one description of the whole task and an indeterminate bar. The note says
  the page can be closed and the design comes back.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — Contract corpus: `running-stage`, `queued-position` valid; `unknown-stage`,
  `queue-position-zero` invalid (TS and Python).
- **AC-002** — Worker: a running job reports its step and forgets it after; queue positions 1 and
  2; a generation reports references → skin → drawing → stencil → placing → finishing in order.
- **AC-003** — Web: `ink-progress.test.ts`.
