# TASK-0016: Multi-Agent Orchestrator with Specialized Research and Visual Creation Agents

## Context and Goal

To guarantee high cultural, historical, and iconographic fidelity and eliminate discrepancies between the technical stencil and the on-skin mockup, the system is architected into a specialized multi-agent system:
1. **Orchestrator Agent (`OrchestratorAgent`)**: Supervises session lifecycle, turn management, and enforces a strict ceiling of at most 3 questions to the client during the discovery phase.
2. **Research Agent (`ResearchAgent`)**: Exclusively researches and extracts authentic iconographic, heraldic, religious, and anatomical motifs (e.g., canonical Mare de Déu dels Desamparats, Real Senyera Coronada, official Valencia CF crest with the bat *rat penat*, calf musculature, black & grey to color transitions). Emits a canonical `TattooTechnicalDossier`.
3. **Visual Creator Agent (`VisualCreatorAgent`)**: Generates the master tattoo artwork strictly based on the dossier and derives the technical stencil directly from the master image, guaranteeing 100% visual identity (1:1 parity) between the stencil and the realistic skin mockup.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-001**: `ResearchAgent` canonical knowledge base accurately identifies and expands historical and cultural motifs without generic or hallucinated imagery.
- **AC-002**: `OrchestratorAgent` strictly limits research questions to a maximum of 3 turns (`questionsAsked <= 3`). If comprehensive details are provided upfront, the research completes immediately without unnecessary questions.
- **AC-003**: `VisualCreatorAgent` derives the technical stencil directly from the master tattoo artwork, ensuring that the lines, layout, and composition of the stencil are 100% identical to the tattoo rendered on human skin.
- **AC-004**: Web interface visibly indicates the speaking agent with customized badges (`Agente Orquestador`, `Agente Investigador`, `Agente Creador Visual`) and tracks question progress (`Pregunta X de 3`).
- **AC-005**: All interactions and labels are strictly in Spanish.
