# Task: A local limit for the AI finish, and a corpus to measure it (audit ARQ-02)

## 1. Problem / requested change

Audit ARQ-02: the AI finish's tolerance was calibrated on one design, and global measures cannot
show whether small details, text, colours or emblems survive. The audit asked for a corpus by
style, colour, text, symbol and skin tone, local metrics as well as global, and false accepts and
rejects reported. Evidence: OBSERVED in `mockup/geometry.py` and ADR-0018.

## 2. Current behavior

`within_tolerance` used the 95th-percentile outline displacement and the ink IoU over the whole
design only.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — `GeometryReport.worst_tile_iou`: the lowest ink IoU over the tiles of a 4 × 4 grid
  where the reference has at least 2 % ink; `GeometryTolerance.min_tile_iou` (0 = unchecked);
  `BLEND_TOLERANCE` sets 0.21.
- **REQ-002** — `mockup.corpus`: four designs (emblem, lettering, fine line, colour) on three skin
  tones, three faithful finish strengths and four redraws (emblem redrawn, emblem dropped, a letter
  changed, the design shifted); `python -m mockup.corpus` prints false accepts and rejects with
  and without the local limit.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — `mockup/tests/test_corpus.py` (light and dark skin): the global limits alone accept
  an emblem redrawn and a letter changed; with the local limit nothing unfaithful passes, and the
  faithful rejections are exactly the global ones.
- **AC-002** — Existing geometry tests unchanged.

## 5. Evidence ledger

- **ev-001** — 2026-10-02. Status: VERIFIED by execution. `python -m mockup.corpus`: global only,
  false accepts **14/39**, false rejects 5/36; global + local, false accepts **0/39**, false
  rejects 5/36 (the same five: colour finishes on medium and dark skin, refused by the global p95
  limit). Faithful worst tile IoU ≥ 0.231; redraws ≤ 0.202. Worker `pytest` **524 passed**;
  `ruff`, `ruff format --check`, `mypy .` clean.
- **NOT_RUN** — Real provider finishes in the corpus (they cost money and vary between runs).
