# Task: Inkcraft by Aurevanta Labs

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner supplied a background (black marble veined in rose gold) and the Aurevanta Labs logo:
"quiero que añadas este fondo a la app, y que también añadas el logo de Aurevanta Labs, que ponga
Inkcraft by Aurevanta Labs, pero que el logo se adapte a la estética de la app". Evidence: INTENT.

TASK-0047 (durable storage) was the planned next step; this was asked first. Evidence: INTENT.

## 2. Current behavior

- The page background is CSS gradients imitating lamplight over ink. Evidence: OBSERVED
  (`globals.css`, TASK-0026).
- The studio header reads "INKCRAFT · Estudio de tatuaje"; the sign-in card reads "INKCRAFT" as an
  eyebrow. The document title and the installable app are named "Tattoo Creator". Evidence:
  OBSERVED.
- The supplied logo is a white wordmark with a symbol in a pink–violet–cyan gradient. Evidence:
  OBSERVED (the PNG, 3200×717, transparent).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — The supplied photograph is the background of every page.
- **REQ-002** — The product reads "Inkcraft by Aurevanta Labs" in the studio header and on the
  sign-in page, and in the document title and the installed app's name.
- **REQ-003** — The Aurevanta symbol and wordmark are recoloured to the app's aesthetic: the rose
  gold of the background's veins, on warm ivory, instead of the original neon gradient.
- **REQ-004** — The sign-in page shows the brand to a visitor who is not signed in.
- **REQ-005** — Text stays legible over the photograph, and a phone shows no horizontal scroll.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — The background, symbol and lockup load on `/entrar` with no session cookie, and on
  the studio page.
- **AC-002** — The page gate does not cover `/brand/*`, and still covers the studio pages.
- **AC-003** — At 375 px the header fits on one line and the page does not overflow sideways.

## 5. Decision log

- **DEC-001** — The rose gold is sampled from the photograph itself (its warm vein pixels span
  roughly `#80594d` to `#f8d5c3`), not chosen by eye, so the mark matches the marble it sits on.
  The recolour keeps the original's lightness per pixel, which is what keeps the symbol's fold
  visible once the hues are gone.
- **DEC-002** — Two uses, not one. The header carries the symbol as a hallmark beside the product
  name, because the full lockup at header height makes "LABS" about four pixels tall. The sign-in
  card has room for the lockup, so there it signs the product: "Inkcraft, by [Aurevanta Labs]".
- **DEC-003** — The photograph and the grain are fixed pseudo-elements at `z-index: -1`, not
  `background-attachment: fixed`, which iOS Safari ignores. Below every page's content, so no page
  has to lift itself above them.
- **DEC-004** — The app's amber accent is left as it is. The request was for the logo to adapt to
  the app, not the reverse.
