# Task: The design keeps the colour asked for, the calf is a calf, and the ink stays on the body

## 1. Problem / requested change

Live run, job `0b3c65d64c184ebf9d5fc81e324a3afd`. The owner wrote: "quiero un tatuaje del estadio de
Mestalla y el escudo del Valencia, con la señera valenciana envolviendo el estadio, hiper realista,
con parte del estadio en blanco y negro y el resto en color, en el gemelo derecho, que ocupe todo
el gemelo". Three failures, and the request "modifica los agentes para que esto no vuelva a
ocurrir", then "el agente que entiende el prompt no lo hace bien". Evidence: INTENT.

## 2. Current behavior

- The stored job brief has `colour.mode: black_and_grey`, while the architect's `style.notes` say
  "mitad del estadio en negro y gris, resto en color". Evidence: OBSERVED (`jobs.sqlite`).
- `researcher.ts` tests "blanco y negro" before "color", so it read black and grey; that value
  counted as explicit and `mergeArchitect` refused the architect's reading. The worker then
  converts any black-and-grey artwork to greyscale (`app/studio.py`, `convert("L")`), so no colour
  could survive. Evidence: OBSERVED.
- The background prompt asked for a "right calf … frontal view"; the generated plate shows the
  front of the lower leg. Evidence: OBSERVED (prompt code; the owner identified the shin).
- Placement assumed skin fills 82 % of the frame (`SKIN_FRAME_FRACTION`); the transform put the
  design at x 157–866 px on a leg that narrows to about 308–744 px, so it ran onto the backdrop.
  Evidence: OBSERVED (job transform; background decrypted locally for inspection).

## 3. Desired behavior

A mixed colour request is kept, the model's reading of the message outranks keyword rules, each
zone's background shows that zone, and the mockup fits the body actually photographed.

## 7. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — A message naming both black-and-grey and colour reads as
  `black_and_grey_with_accent`; a negated colour ("sin color") does not count as colour.
- **REQ-002** — Where the architect answered, its reading replaces the keyword rules' reading of
  the same message for style, colour and zone. Panel choices are never reread; side and size stay
  the client's (TASK-0033 rules unchanged for them).
- **REQ-003** — The architect prompt says colour mode decides colour (realism in colour is
  `black_and_grey_realism` + `colour`) and that a whole-zone request gets the whole zone's size;
  the artwork prompt says the colour mode wins over the realism style's name.
- **REQ-004** — The background prompt names each zone's camera view; the calf is the back of the
  lower leg seen from behind; nothing asks for a frontal view.
- **REQ-005** — The mockup measures the body silhouette, centres the design on it, takes the
  cylinder's taper from it, shrinks the design until at most 2 % of its ink falls outside
  (never below 60 %), and prints no ink or redness on the backdrop. A calibrated photo keeps its
  scale. With no readable body, nothing changes.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — The owner's exact sentence extracts `black_and_grey_with_accent`; single and
  negated cues keep their old readings.
- **AC-002** — With an architect proposing the mixed mode for that message, the session adopts it,
  keeps the client's side "right" and fits the size to the calf.
- **AC-003** — `textReadings` excludes fields chosen in the panel.
- **AC-004** — Every contract zone has a camera view; the calf prompt says "from directly behind"
  and contains no "frontal".
- **AC-005** — On a synthetic leg the unfitted composite spills over 1000 backdrop pixels and the
  fitted one spills none; the design centres on a shifted leg; a calibrated photo keeps its width;
  a uniform photo is unchanged byte for byte.
- **AC-006** — Re-compositing the real job's master on its real background reproduces the old
  placement exactly without the fit, and keeps the design on the leg with it.

## 15. Decision log

- **DEC-001** — Supersedes TASK-0033/AC-002 for style, colour and zone. That rule let keyword
  matches beat the model, on the reasoning that a model should not override what the client said.
  Both read the same sentence; the regex reads it worse, and the live run proved it. Panel values
  are still never overridden, and the side and size rules are unchanged.
- **DEC-002** — The keyword fix is kept alongside DEC-001, because the architect is opt-in and may
  be unavailable; the deterministic path must not fail the same way.
- **DEC-003** — Clipping at the silhouette is physical rather than cosmetic: ink past the outline of
  a limb wraps round it, out of the camera's sight. The design is shrunk first so the clip removes
  only that wrapped margin; the stencil and master are untouched (MOCKUP-INV-001).
- **DEC-004** — The silhouette is chroma against a plain backdrop, not depth or a model. Our own
  backgrounds are prompted with a neutral strip on both sides for this reason. A low-confidence
  reading returns nothing and the old framing applies.
- **DEC-005** — scipy was not used: it ships no stubs and the worker's mypy is strict. The same
  operations use scikit-image and PIL, already dependencies.
- **DEC-006** — Out of scope: the AI blend of ADR-0016. It exists as a graph node with no provider
  wired and runs only on an own photo; activating it needs a provider choice, a numeric geometry
  tolerance and paid measurement runs. The body fit is a prerequisite for it either way: a blend may
  not move geometry, so a design placed off the leg would stay off the leg.
