# Task: Designs last

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner wants an administrator who can see what users have made, and chose to make designs
durable first ("Primero TASK-0047"). Offered a full move of the design store to Supabase or a fix of
the lifecycle in place, they chose the latter ("sigue con la propuesta"). Evidence: INTENT.

## 2. Current behavior

- Every stored file used `RetentionClass.PHOTO`, so designs expired after 24 hours, contrary to
  MEDIA-INV-006. Evidence: OBSERVED (`app/studio.py`, three `store_artifact` sites and
  `store_vector`).
- Design files descended from the body photo or the first reference, both photographs, so even a
  design-class file would have been cascade-deleted when they expired. Evidence: OBSERVED.
- History was windowed to 24 hours and the purge deleted every finished job older than a day.
  Evidence: OBSERVED (`jobs/queue.py`, `Studio.purge_expired`).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — A design and every file that shows no person outlives the 24-hour window, stays
  listed in history, and opens.
- **REQ-002** — A body photograph, a kept camera photo, and whatever was composed on them still
  expire after 24 hours and are erased with everything derived from them.
- **REQ-003** — A version whose skin view expired still opens and says why the view is gone; a
  change asked from it is refused with that reason.
- **REQ-004** — A kept camera photo's version goes with its photo; failed and cancelled runs are
  swept; successful versions are not.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — Purged 25 hours later, a generated-plate design's files, reference and version
  remain; its master has no expiry.
- **AC-002** — Purged 25 hours later, an own-photo design loses the photo, mockup and background,
  and keeps master, stencils and PDF, and its version.
- **AC-003** — An edit of that version answers 422 with the expiry reason.
- **AC-004** — A capture version disappears with its photo; its parent stays.
- **AC-005** — A failed run older than 24 hours is removed.
- **AC-006** — `ingest_photo` accepts only the photo or design class.
- **AC-007** — In the studio, a version whose skin view is gone shows the notice instead of a broken
  image, and the history note describes the new lifecycle.

## 5. Decision log

- **DEC-001** — ADR-0023 records the lifecycle by likeness and refines MEDIA-INV-006.
- **DEC-002** — References take the design class: the gate admits a person only in an own-body
  photo with consent, so a reference shows nobody.
- **DEC-003** — Existing files keep the expiry they were written with. No migration: they are demo
  data, and rewriting encrypted-store metadata in production to save a day of it is not worth the
  risk.
