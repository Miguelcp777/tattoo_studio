# Task: A large stencil also prints on A4, at 1:1 (audit, section 5)

## 1. Problem / requested change

The audit's product gaps (section 5, «Impresión profesional») recommend A4/A3 tiling with printer
margins. The stencil PDF had one page the size of the design plus margins: a 150 × 300 mm calf
design does not fit A4, so a home printer set to A4 shrinks or crops it. Evidence: OBSERVED in
`stencil/engine.py` (`export_pdf`).

## 2. Current behavior

One custom-size page.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — When the design with its margins does not fit A4, the PDF (and its mirror) adds A4
  pages after the full-size one: pieces of 190 × 257 mm at 1:1 with a 10 mm overlap, corner marks,
  the piece's position («A4 piece n of m», row and column) and the 50 mm calibration bar.
- **REQ-002** — The viewer says so beside the downloads when it applies.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — `test_a_design_larger_than_a4_also_prints_in_a4_pieces`: grid sizes; a 150 × 300
  mm design gives the full page plus two A4 pages; an 80 × 150 mm design one page.
- **AC-002** — `print-pieces.test.ts`.
- **AC-003** — A rendered sample.

## 5. Evidence ledger

- **ev-001** — 2026-10-02. Status: VERIFIED by execution. Worker `pytest` **525 passed**; web
  `vitest` **169 passed**; `ruff`, `mypy`, `tsc`, ESLint clean. A 150 × 300 mm sample rendered with
  pdf.js in the in-app browser: page 1 the full design, pages 2 and 3 the A4 pieces with corner
  marks, labels and the calibration bar; the second piece starts 10 mm above the end of the first.
