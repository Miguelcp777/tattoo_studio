# Task: Whole-zone coverage resolves in millimetres

## 1. Problem / requested change

A user asking for a tattoo that covers their whole calf gets, at best, a marginally larger picture
and an unchanged stencil. At a realistic calf size they get nothing at all. Implement ADR-0008.

## 2. Current behavior

- `fit_coverage` caps coverage at `min(0.82, 0.82 * aspect / ratio)`, a fraction of the image
  frame. `auto` shares the ceiling. Evidence: VERIFIED 2026-09-22, measured against the real
  1024x1536 background geometry; at 360 mm requested height `auto` and `full` return an identical
  width for ink aspects 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5 and 3.0.
- `coverage_request` matches a whitelist. `que cubra el gemelo entero`, `que me coja todo el
  gemelo`, `de la rodilla al tobillo`, `que ocupe toda la pierna de arriba a abajo` and `que ocupe
  todo el brazo` all return `(None, False)`. Evidence: VERIFIED 2026-09-22.
- `reposition` reuses the parent's stencil and print assets verbatim and emits a notice stating the
  PDF millimetres are unchanged. Evidence: OBSERVED, `app/studio.py`.
- Only a 150 dpi raster of the vector master is persisted, so no exact resize path exists.
  Evidence: OBSERVED, `app/studio.py` stores `rasterize(master)` as `master`.

## 3. Desired behavior

A coverage request naming a body zone resolves the brief's millimetres from declared reference
anatomy, re-exports the stencil at the new size, and tells the user the new size. Nudge controls
stay visual-only. No generation call is made for either.

## 4. Scope

`services/worker/mockup/anatomy.py` (new), `services/worker/mockup/placement.py`,
`services/worker/stencil/engine.py`, `services/worker/app/studio.py`, `apps/web` notice surface,
and the tests for each.

## 5. Out of scope

Body segmentation and depth estimation. Asking the user for their own measurements. Any change to
`contracts`. Closing TASK-0019, TASK-0020 or TASK-0023.

## 6. Affected anchors

ADR-0008 (implements), ADR-0007 (vector master authoritative — honoured more closely, not changed),
ADR-0003 (no re-tracing of traced output), MOCKUP-INV-003, PROD-INV-001, TASK-0022 (narrowed).

## 7. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — `mockup/anatomy.py` declares a usable ink span in millimetres for every member of
  the `bodyPart` enum, each within the contract's 5–600 mm bounds, labelled reference anatomy.
- **REQ-002** — `zone_size(body_part, ink_aspect)` fits the artwork aspect inside the zone span
  preserving aspect, maximising coverage, and clamping to contract bounds.
- **REQ-003** — Whole-zone intent is matched by vocabulary. The five phrases listed in §2 resolve
  to whole-zone coverage. The Spanish noun is not mapped onto the `bodyPart` enum; the brief's
  declared `placement.bodyPart` is used.
- **REQ-004** — A whole-zone request sets `brief.size` from REQ-002 and increments the brief
  revision.
- **REQ-005** — The vector `Master` is persisted alongside the raster, and a resize is an exact
  vector scale of it. No re-trace of already-traced output occurs on a resize.
- **REQ-006** — The stencil SVG, its mirror and the PDF are re-exported at the new millimetres.
- **REQ-007** — No coverage revision, zone or nudge, issues a generation provider call.
- **REQ-008** — Nudge coverage (`larger`, `smaller`) leaves `brief.size` and every print asset
  unchanged.
- **REQ-009** — The notice states the resulting millimetres, and does not claim the PDF is
  unchanged when it changed.
- **REQ-010** — A calibrated placement (`photoWidthMm`) takes precedence over the reference table.
- **REQ-011** — For a zone request the rendered projection differs from `auto` at every requested
  size; the `full == auto` collapse measured in §2 does not occur.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** (REQ-001) — A test asserts every `bodyPart` enum member has a span, and that every
  span lies within 5–600 mm. Enum read from the schema, not restated.
- **AC-002** (REQ-002) — Fitting a 1:3 and a 3:1 artwork into `calf` both preserve aspect to within
  0.1 %, fit inside the span, and touch at least one span bound.
- **AC-003** (REQ-003) — The five phrases from §2 resolve to whole-zone intent; `lo quiero mas
  grande` still resolves to a nudge; a mixed artwork+coverage request still retains the artwork
  edit.
- **AC-004** (REQ-004) — A zone request on a calf brief produces a brief whose `heightMm` equals
  the calf span height within rounding, and whose revision is the parent's plus one.
- **AC-005** (REQ-005, REQ-006) — After a zone resize the exported SVG declares the new mm, and the
  scaled vector paths are geometrically similar to the parent's to within 0.1 %.
- **AC-006** (REQ-007) — A recording provider asserts zero calls across a zone revision and a nudge
  revision.
- **AC-007** (REQ-008) — A nudge revision leaves `brief.size` and the stencil asset bytes identical.
- **AC-008** (REQ-009) — The emitted notice contains the new millimetres and does not contain the
  unchanged-PDF claim when the size changed.
- **AC-009** (REQ-010) — A zone request on a calibrated placement does not consult the table.
- **AC-010** (REQ-011) — Re-running the §2 measurement over the same five ink aspects at 200, 300
  and 360 mm shows a zone request differing from `auto` in every cell.

## 9. Design approach

See ADR-0008. The reference table is the only new convention; everything else is plumbing that
follows from resolving size before projection rather than after.

## 10. API / data / UI impact

One new persisted artifact per design (the vector master, JSON). One new worker module. The web
surface renders the notice it is given; the millimetres it displays come from the brief.

## 11. Migration and backward compatibility

Parents stored before this task have no vector master. A zone resize on such a parent falls back to
the reference table for size but cannot produce an exact vector scale; it reports that the stencil
was not resized rather than re-tracing. Nudge revisions on legacy parents are unaffected.

## 12. Test plan

Unit tests per module. The §2 measurement is re-run as a regression test so the `full == auto`
collapse cannot return. Studio-level tests cover the zone and nudge paths with a recording
provider.

## 13. Risks and rollback

The printed deliverable changes from a chat sentence — the main risk, accepted in ADR-0008 and
mitigated by stating the new size and by versioning. Rollback is reverting to nudge-only
resolution; the reference table and the persisted vector master are independently useful.

## 15. Decision log

- **DEC-001** — The Spanish noun is not mapped onto the `bodyPart` enum. The brief already
  declares the body part; inferring whether "pierna" means `calf` or `thigh_front` would invent an
  answer the brief holds. The phrase establishes intent only. A test asserts this directly: its
  instructions say «gemelo» while the brief declares `inner_forearm`, and the brief wins.
- **DEC-002** — The vector master is kept in an internal `design_vector` table rather than added
  to `studio-status.schema.json`. It is authoritative geometry, not a client artifact, and
  widening the client contract for an internal need would be the wrong trade. This also kept
  `contracts` out of the task's scope as declared in §5.
- **DEC-003** — A parent with no stored vector keeps its millimetres instead of resizing. §11
  originally proposed resizing anyway and reporting that the stencil was not resized; that would
  leave the brief and the printed sheet disagreeing, which is worse than not acting. Corrected
  during implementation.
- **DEC-004** — Fitting a zone can *shrink* a design. A square design cannot cover a calf top to
  bottom, and the alternative — distorting the artwork — is forbidden by MOCKUP-INV-001. This was
  found by a test written to assert the opposite, and the acceptance criterion was corrected to
  what is true rather than the behaviour being bent to the criterion.
- **DEC-005** — The widening of auto placement beyond `calf` is gated behind a zone request.
  Widening it unconditionally changed the mockup for every non-calf brief, which three existing
  tests caught; that is scope this task has no mandate for.
