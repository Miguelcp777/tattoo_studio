# Task: While an agent works, a hand tattoos

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner asked for a progress indicator from the world of tattooing, such as a hand tattooing,
whenever an agent is working, so the client never thinks the app has frozen, in the studio's
style. Until now the guided steps only changed a button's label («Preparando tu propuesta…») while
the idea was studied, which can take tens of seconds, and generation showed a plain moving bar.
Evidence: INTENT; OBSERVED (`IdeaStep.tsx`, `GenerationProgress.tsx`).

## 2. Current behavior

`run()` in `app/page.tsx` sets `busy` for every server call; the steps disable their answers.
`GenerationProgress` showed a phase label, an indeterminate bar and the time spent. Evidence: OBSERVED.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — While an agent works, the studio shows `InkWorking`: a gloved hand with a pen
  machine tattooing a rose on skin, the needle following the line as it is inked; the task's title,
  a changing line about its stages, a bar and the time spent. Rose-gold and dark, like the studio.
- **REQ-002** — It is shown in every guided step while that step waits (the idea, the details,
  the references, the summary), during generation (`GenerationProgress`, guided and advanced) and
  in the advanced chat. A wait under 400 ms does not show it; it scrolls itself into sight.
- **REQ-003** — It says nothing it does not know. No provider reports progress: the lines are the
  task's stages on a timer and stay on the last one; the bar follows the time against the task's
  usual time, never goes back and never passes 94 %. Screen readers hear the title, not every line.
- **REQ-004** — With reduced motion the rose is finished and the hand still; the text still moves.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — `lib/ink-progress.test.ts`: every task starts at its first line with an empty bar;
  lines advance and stay on the last; the bar is monotonic and capped; the clock reads m:ss.
- **AC-002** — Live: submitting an idea shows the hand, its title, a changing line, the bar and
  the clock inside the step-1 pop-up, at 375 px and on the desktop, with no console error.
- **AC-003** — Live: the details step and the advanced chat show it while they wait.
- **AC-004** — Live, with `prefers-reduced-motion` answering true: the rose is finished, the SVG has
  no animation element, and the title and clock still show.
