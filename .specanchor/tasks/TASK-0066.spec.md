# Task: The header stays usable in the guided studio; it always starts guided

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner: "En modo guiado no veo la opción de salir"; and "al entrar a la app, la primera opción
siempre es el modo guiado, pero el poder cambiar a modo avanzado tiene que ser bien visible".
Evidence: INTENT.

## 2. Current behavior

The wizard's pop-ups were modal `<dialog>`s, which make the rest of the page inert: the header's
«Salir», «Panel» and «Modo avanzado» showed but could not be pressed. At 375 px the header did not
wrap and pushed «Salir» off the screen. The mode was remembered in `localStorage`, so a visit could
start in advanced mode, and the switch was a small text link. Evidence: OBSERVED.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — With a wizard pop-up open, the header (mode switch, «Panel», «Salir») is visible and
  can be pressed, on a phone too.
- **REQ-002** — Every visit starts in guided mode; the choice lasts for the tab only.
- **REQ-003** — The mode switch is a prominent control in the header.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — `reading-order.test.ts`: every wizard `<Confirm>` is `belowHeader`; header z-index >
  pop-up > backdrop.
- **AC-002** — Live at 375 px: with step 1 open, the switch, «Panel» and «Salir» are inside the
  viewport and are the element at their own centre; no horizontal overflow; «Salir» signs out to
  `/entrar`.

## 5. Evidence ledger

- **ev-001** — 2026-10-01. Status: VERIFIED by execution. Web `vitest` **129 passed** (127 before);
  `tsc`, ESLint, Prettier clean; `next build` succeeded.
- **ev-002** — 2026-10-01, `node scripts/dev.mjs` with the local stand-ins, 375 × 812. Status:
  VERIFIED by execution (measured in the page). No modal dialog open; the switch («⚙ Modo
  avanzado», a pill), «Panel» and «Salir» in view and hit-tested as the topmost element; no
  overflow. Clicking «Salir» signed out and landed on `/entrar`. A first click sent through the
  browser pane while it was hidden did not arrive; the in-page click did.
