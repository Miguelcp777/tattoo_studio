# Task: A backdrop painted by the model never becomes ink

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner: «¿por qué me ha generado esa zona gris alrededor?». A biomechanical design came back
inside a grey gradient card; the compositor treats everything darker than white as ink, so the
card was tattooed onto the thigh as a grey panel (trapezoidal after the body fit) and traced into
the stencil. Evidence: OBSERVED (screenshots); the cause OBSERVED in `mockup/engine.py`
(`visible_artwork`, ink = not white).

## 2. Current behavior

The artwork prompt asked for «pure white» only in passing; nothing checked or cleaned the ground.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — `stencil/backdrop.clear_backdrop`: a smooth, light or mid-toned region connected to
  the image border and covering at least 15 % of it is turned to white before the artwork is traced
  or composed. A white ground and a dark field (blackwork) are left unchanged.
- **REQ-002** — `make_artwork` applies it to every artwork, new or edited.
- **REQ-003** — The colour prompt says everything outside the motif is #FFFFFF, with no panel,
  card, tinted rectangle, vignette or gradient, and that effects such as torn skin fade into white.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — Stencil tests: a grey gradient card is cleared while the motif's core and soft
  shading stay; white and dark grounds return the same bytes.
- **AC-002** — `test_a_backdrop_painted_by_the_model_never_reaches_the_design` on the shaded path
  passes with the change and fails without it.
- **AC-003** — The colour prompt contains the new instruction.
