# Task: A job accepts every style the brief does

## 1. Problem / requested change

On the deployed studio the owner prepared a biomechanical design on the right thigh, attached a
reference, accepted the brief and pressed "Generar diseño y plantilla". Every attempt answered
"Revisa las referencias, la posición y las medidas." The owner: "añado referencias y configuro todo
pero sigo recibiendo el mensaje". Evidence: INTENT (screenshot).

## 2. Current behavior

`apps/web/src/app/api/generate/route.ts` validates the job against `studio-job` and answers that
message on any failure. `studio-job` embeds its own copy of the brief. TASK-0027 grew `styleName` in
`tattoo-brief` from ten to sixteen styles and left the copy at ten, so `biomechanical`, `chicano`,
`geometric`, `new_school`, `tribal` and `watercolour` briefs are valid, reach "ready to generate",
and are then refused. Evidence: VERIFIED (ev-001).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — A job accepts every style the brief accepts.
- **REQ-002** — The brief embedded in `studio-job`, and the definitions it shares with
  `tattoo-brief`, cannot differ from `tattoo-brief` without a failing test.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — A job whose brief is biomechanical, black and grey with an accent, on the right front
  thigh at 129 x 272 mm, validates in TypeScript and Python (`valid/widened-style.json`).
