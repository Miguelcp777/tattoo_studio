# Task: Mockup blend spike — geometry tolerance and blend-provider selection

Planned at bootstrap (2026-09-19) and cited by ADR-0002, ADR-0016, FINDING-0001 and the mockup,
generation and orchestration specs, but never specified until now. Prepared on 2026-09-26 at the
user's request; execution is **blocked** on inputs only the user can provide (§ Blockers).

## 1. Problem / requested change

ADR-0016 activated the constrained AI blend, but nothing can be accepted until three things are
known: how to measure whether a blend moved the design, the numeric limit, and which provider (if
any) stays inside it while being allowed to process consented body photos without retaining them.

## 6. Method (delivered)

`services/worker/mockup/geometry.py`:

1. Compare only the design region: the composite's recorded `xPx/yPx/widthPx/heightPx` + 10% margin.
2. Normalise each image against a heavy blur of itself (removes lighting/tone), find ink as pixels
   markedly darker than the surrounding skin, drop specks.
3. Symmetric nearest-edge distance between ink outlines; report the **95th percentile** in pixels
   and relative to the design diagonal, plus ink-mask IoU. A result at another resolution is
   scaled back first; the distortion that introduces is itself measured.
4. Fail closed: no ink in the reference, or ink gone from the result, reports infinite distance.

Findings from the synthetic tests: skin integration (relight, retone, texture, soften, other
resolution) measures ≈0 px; a 3 px shift measures 3 px; a ~20 px shift 19.7 px; a removed element
36 px. **IoU alone does not catch a removed element (0.82) and drops fast for small shifts on thin
lines (0.62 at 3 px)** — displacement must be the primary criterion, IoU a secondary guard.

## 7. Calibration protocol (to run)

1. For each corpus photo, build the geometric warp with `composite`.
2. Apply known-good perturbations (relight, retone, texture, blur up to the strongest a real blend
   shows) and known-bad ones (shifts of 1, 2, 3 mm at the zone's scale; one element removed; one
   added). Record the metric for each.
3. Choose `max_p95_relative` and `min_ink_iou` as the pair that accepts every known-good and rejects
   every known-bad case, with margin. If no pair separates them on real photos, the method is
   inadequate: stop and revise it rather than loosen the limit.
4. Run each candidate provider; record pass rate, p95 distribution per skin tone / zone / lighting,
   latency, cost, refusals.
5. Blind A/B of blended vs geometric-only by at least three raters.

## 9. Corpus rules

Consented or synthetic images only, never retained user uploads (ADR-0002, mockup spec). At least
five skin tones (light to deep), four zones (forearm, calf, back, chest), three lighting conditions.
Photos stay **outside the repository**; the manifest records only ids, attributes and consent
record references. A synthetic option exists: the FLUX.2 skin-background generator already used by
the studio (ADR-0009) can produce consent-free body photos, at API cost.
