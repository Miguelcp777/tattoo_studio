# Task: The design fills the zone, and the plate is a man's or a woman's body

## 1. Problem / requested change

Owner: "afina la cobertura, y también debemos considerar si el gemelo es de un hombre o de una
mujer". The live Mestalla mockup left bare calf below the design, and the generated leg had no
stated sex. Evidence: INTENT.

Answered when asked: the sex is captured **both** ways — the agent proposes it and the client can
set it — and when it is unknown the client is **asked** (it blocks acceptance) rather than defaulted.

## 2. Current behavior

- `_fit_to_body` shrinks a whole-zone design until at most `MAX_SPILL` (0.02) of its ink falls
  outside the silhouette. On the Mestalla calf that meant scale 0.63: the design stopped near the
  floor with bare skin below. Evidence: OBSERVED (`bodyFit.scale=0.63` in the live transform).
- Nothing carries the presented sex of the body; `background_prompt` says "adult". Evidence: OBSERVED.

## 15. Decision log

- **DEC-001** — `MAX_SPILL` 0.02 -> 0.06. A limb wraps ~180 degrees, so the outermost columns of a
  full-width design continue around its side, out of the camera's sight; a small margin there lets
  a whole-zone design fill the zone. Checked on the real render at up to 12 %: the clip keeps the
  edge clean. 6 % fills well.
- **DEC-002** — `bodyType` lives in `placement`, because it qualifies the body the placement is on.
  It is added to both the tattoo-brief and the studio-job schemas: the job embeds its own copy of
  the brief, and the first fix (brief only) made the worker reject the job (422). It never enters
  `build_prompt` (the artwork), only `background_prompt`.
- **DEC-003** — `bodyType` blocks acceptance (master brief `missing`) but is not added to
  `missingPreferences`, so it is asked via the panel rather than a fourth chat question (the cap is
  three). Readiness of the chat phase is unchanged; only the acceptance gate requires it.
- **DEC-004** — Unlike `side`, `bodyType` is merged from the architect: the owner asked the agent to
  propose it. `side` stays the client's alone.
- **DEC-005** — Not addressed: an own photograph already is the body, so `bodyType` will not be
  required once own-photo generation is active (ADR-0007). Today generation is on generated plates.
