# Task: A catalogue pick sets the style and is never sent as a reference

## 1. Problem / requested change

In live use, generation failed with "La imagen no ha superado la revisión de contenido". The owner
then asked: "no quiero incluir la foto como referencia, solo quiero que el usuario pueda elegir qué
estilo de los mostrados le gusta y que el tatuaje sea de ese estilo". Evidence: INTENT.

## 2. Current behavior

- `style_variant` added the pick to `references` as `style_library` (ADR-0012 point 6), and
  `POST /api/generate` uploaded its bytes through `catalogueBytes` (TASK-0030). Evidence: OBSERVED.
- In the failing session the worker log shows two `/studio/media` 200s and a 422, in the order of
  the three references, the third being the catalogue pick. Evidence: OBSERVED (preview logs).
- The gate prompt counts "any recognisable body part such as a hand, arm or face" as a person, and
  the chosen render shows a forearm. That this was the rejection reason is INFERRED: the worker
  does not log the reason code, and the classification was not re-run.

## 3. Desired behavior

The pick sets the style, is shown in the brief, and never leaves the web app.

## 7. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — `style_variant` sets `slots.style.primary` and `session.stylePick`, and leaves
  `references` untouched. An unknown identifier is refused.
- **REQ-002** — The master brief names the picked variant while the style still matches, and does
  not count the pick as a reference.
- **REQ-003** — No catalogue image is uploaded to the worker; only real references are.
- **REQ-004** — A pick is forgotten when the style moves to another one.
- **REQ-005** — With no reference at all, the client is sent to «Referencias» (upload or search),
  not to the style picker, which can no longer resolve it.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — After a pick, the style is set, `stylePick` is recorded and `references` equal what
  they were before.
- **AC-002** — The brief's style line reads "Neotradicional · Animal"; the references line ignores
  the pick; a pick from another style is not shown.
- **AC-003** — Accept and generate: the worker receives exactly one upload (the real reference) and
  a brief with `style.primary: neo_traditional`.
- **AC-004** — Changing the style drops the pick; keeping it keeps the pick.
- **AC-005** — The no-reference message and blocker point to «Referencias».
- **AC-006** — Planting the old behaviour (adding the pick to `references`) fails the test.

## 15. Decision log

- **DEC-001** — The variant's `characteristics` are not sent to the generator. They describe a
  subject as much as a style ("stylised animal portrait"), which would push a tiger into a crest.
  The owner's rule is to leave the models their creative room; the style phrase already reaches the
  prompt through `brief.style.primary`.
- **DEC-002** — A pick no longer counts as the "referencia visual" readiness requires, because the
  job contract requires at least one reference and uses it as the lineage parent (ADR-0017,
  Consequences). Relaxing that is a contract and privacy-lineage change, left to its own task.
- **DEC-003** — `catalogueBytes` and `isCatalogueSource` are deleted rather than kept unused;
  `referenceBytes` still refuses a catalogue path, which the test pins, so a pick left over in an
  older in-memory session cannot be uploaded either.
